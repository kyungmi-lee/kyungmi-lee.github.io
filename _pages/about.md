I'm an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Rice University. 
I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2024, advised by Prof. Anantha P. Chandrakasan. 
Prior to joining Rice, I was a postdoctoral associate at the Research Laboratory of Electronics at MIT. 

My research focus is on computer architecture and VLSI, where I design hardware support for secure and trustworthy AI computing and develop frameworks to understand evolving AI hardware. 
Through my research, I aim to advance future AI computing platforms that protect privacy and integrity while delivering high performance and energy efficiency.
